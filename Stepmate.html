Stepmate
/App/
Stepmate is a walking companion app that encourages users to stay active by matching them with people to walk together.

Context
In the digital age, people are becoming increasingly sedentary due to office work, prolonged computer use, mobile entertainment, and a lack of daily movement. Although many health apps are available, most focus primarily on tracking metrics such as step count and calories burned, while lacking features that effectively motivate users to build and maintain long-term walking habits.
Problem
How might we help users stay motivated and maintain a consistent walking habit without giving up halfway?
Solution
A walking app that tracks users steps and allows them to share progress with friends. It features daily and weekly step leaderboards, group challenges, shared streaks to stay motivated together, and most importantly, a matching feature that connects users with new walking companions.
