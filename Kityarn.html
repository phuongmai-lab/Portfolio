Problem
Users often choose yarn based on intuition rather than project needs.
Yarn product descriptions are often generic and difficult to understand.
There is no clear guidance on choosing the right yarn for specific projects.
Solution
A website that guides crocheters to the most suitable yarn based on a simple question flow.
Business Goals
Short-term
- Sell yarn through a personalized yarn recommendation experience.
- Build trust by providing recommendations based on users' real needs.
Long-term
- Build a complete yarn ecosystem for Kityarn.
- Position Kityarn as the brand that understands crocheters best.
